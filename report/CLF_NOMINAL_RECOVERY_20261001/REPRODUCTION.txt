Development evidence, excluded from final campaign counts
--------------------------------------------------------
- campaign-final is an earlier CLF-only implementation: eleven completed
  exports include three sampled collisions and a model-domain failure.
  Three in-flight cases were interrupted. These failures motivated the
  positive-slack predictive-cost tie-break and the common 5-ms mesh.
- campaign-refined showed that 5-ms refinement alone could still miss a
  short curvedCrossing contact at 8 m/s. Remaining processes were interrupted
  to implement adaptive interval checks; those outputs are not final results.
- campaign-interval-prefix exposed an empty-cut-vector dimension bug.
  campaign-interval-prefix-fixed then exposed unchecked prefix-cache reuse.
  Final campaign-interval uses corrected certification flags and the same
  later-input correction for transferred trajectories that lose admission.
- campaign-interval is the preceding interval-certified implementation. It
  contains twelve recovered cases, one 8-m/s turningCrossing model-domain
  failure at 11.05 s, and an 8-m/s curvedHeadOn run without a five-second
  recovery dwell by the 80-second observation cap. Four focused prefixes
  were independently audited then resumed with unchanged source/settings.
  These results exposed extreme first-step Newton correction and motivated
  applying the existing input trust box to that correction.
- trustPilot.m starts from the same old 4-second turningCrossing snapshot
  to isolate the bounded correction. It recovers at 28.45 s with minimum
  independently sampled distance 6.81948 mm; the unbounded implementation
  fails at 11.05 s. This paired continuation is extra diagnostic evidence,
  not one of the fourteen final cold-start cases. The target approaches
  again near nine seconds rather than departing permanently.
- campaign-trust is an intermediate bounded-correction version. Its cold
  8-m/s crossing case fails at 5 s when a shifted open-loop tail leaves the
  Fiala chart. The actual initial state remains evaluable. Three remaining
  intermediate workers were terminated after the cause was isolated; their
  partial logs remain and are not counted as complete runs. See
  trust-campaign-interruption.json. A captured-state diagnostic with the
  added nonlinear-defect retraction returns a fully admitted zero-safety
  witness under the original budget (projectionFailure.log).
- campaign-projection is the intermediate version that applied defect
  retraction only to unevaluable rollouts. Thirteen runs recovered, while the
  8-m/s curvedCrossing rollout remained safe but diverged temporarily (at 10 s,
  W=58494.9 and lateral error=-22.3957 m). Its remaining run was manually
  interrupted as superseded; projection-campaign-interruption.json records
  the actual last log lines. This is not a completed final case. The final
  implementation also offers retraction to a defined but inadmissible full
  conic proposal whose first stage is admissible, preserving the raw point.
  The final cold curvedCrossing trial has W=12.0658 and lateral error=-0.184219 m
  at 10 s, and confirms recovery at 17.9 s with 9.86736-mm minimum sampled gap.
- sameFrame.m/clfFinalInternals.m isolate CLF correction at four states from
  the prior causal study, retaining their original 8/10-hold horizons and

  all four produce admitted zero-slack witnesses (same-frame.json).
  This probe precedes cost tie-breaking and adaptive interval checks; its
  timings are not final campaign timings.
