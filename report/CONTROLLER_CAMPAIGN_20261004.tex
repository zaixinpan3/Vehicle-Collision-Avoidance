\textbf{Purpose.} This rerun checks the current controller on the fourteen
collision-threat encounters after the observer-integration commits
(838848b, 388dcfc, 8584c47) that followed the 20-cm margin campaign
(\path{MARGIN_20CM_20261003.tex}). No source or configuration was changed.

\textbf{Setup.} The source is HEAD 8584c47 with no modification to
\path{controller/}, \path{config/} or \path{scripts/}. The only untracked
script, \path{scripts/runObserverComparisonCampaign.m}, is not called. The
command was run from the repository root:
\begin{verbatim}
matlab -batch "addpath('scripts'); runPotentialFieldCampaign(pwd,
  '<repo>/report/CONTROLLER_CAMPAIGN_20261004',1500)"
\end{verbatim}
It covers seven encounters (headOn, acceleratingHeadOn, brakingLead, crossing,
turningCrossing, curvedHeadOn, curvedCrossing) at 8 and 15 m/s, with horizon 8
or 16 holds and a 1500-hold observation cap. It requires a baseline collision
threat and a 1-s nominal-recovery dwell. The required clearance is 0.20 m.
Observations are exact target states, so no random draws occur. The plant is an
\texttt{ode45} replay, and no computation delay is injected.

\textbf{Outcome.} 12 of 14 encounters avoid and recover. There are 0 sampled
collisions and 0 path-limit violations over 3934 issued holds. Two 8-m/s
cases stop with
\texttt{noOptimizationSolution (linearizationAccuracyNotReached)}:
turningCrossing at hold 20 (1.0 s, before the encounter, gap 2.89 m) and
curvedCrossing at hold 219 (10.95 s, during recovery after passing, minimum
gap 0.138 m). A stopped run is not counted as a success. Both cases recovered
in the 20-cm margin campaign, where only 15-m/s acceleratingHeadOn was
unrecovered.

Recovery is the time at which the nominal-recovery dwell was confirmed. Six
executed scenes have a sampled gap below the configured 0.20 m: 8-m/s
brakingLead, crossing and curvedCrossing, and 15-m/s headOn and
acceleratingHeadOn. The margin is a node-sampled planning buffer, not a
continuous-time guarantee.

\textbf{Measured runtime.} This is a shared workstation without warm-up
isolation. Per-case median running frames are 19.5--42.7 ms and per-case p95
frames are 38.0--94.7 ms. The largest frame is 853 ms, the first call of the
first case (MATLAB warm-up). Excluding that call, the largest frame is
200.6 ms (15-m/s headOn first call). No real-time guarantee is asserted.
