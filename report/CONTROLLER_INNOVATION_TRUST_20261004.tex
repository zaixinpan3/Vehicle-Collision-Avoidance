\section{Changes}
Following the user's direction, the controller now issues every completed
PCBF/CLF solution directly. The nonlinear replay of each solution, its pose,
state, CLF and path-deviation agreement test, the damped step, the line search,
the retry triggered by disagreement and the three prediction tolerances are
deleted. Fallbacks for a \emph{failed} solve are kept: the bounded input-box
enlargement and the single fresh potential-field seed.

This removes the information that the old code used to adapt the input trust
scale $\Delta$. A fixed value was tried first (Section~\ref{sec:fixed}). The
adopted alternative estimates $\Delta$ from the next posterior.

\paragraph{Plan innovation.}
For a correction of size $s$ (largest input change from the anchor, in units
of the box $\pm r[0.15;0.25]$), the affine prediction differs from the nonlinear
rollout of the same inputs by a second-order remainder $e\approx L s^2$. Let
$\hat x$ be the previous plan's affine prediction of nodes $2..N{+}1$ and
$\bar x$ the next anchor, which is the shifted rollout from the new posterior.
A nonlinear replay $\tilde x$ of the same inputs from the plan's own prediction
$\tilde x_1=\hat x_1$ splits the innovation exactly:
\[
\bar x-\hat x=(\bar x-\tilde x)+(\tilde x-\hat x).
\]
The first term propagates the posterior's departure from the prediction and
belongs to the observer. The second contains no posterior. With the safety
body-point metric $P(\delta x)=\|\delta p\|+r_E|\delta\psi|$, define
$e_k=\max_j P(\tilde x_j-\hat x_j)$. Then
\[
\hat L_k=\max\!\Big(\frac{e_k}{\max(s_{k-1},\Delta_{k-1}/2)^2},\ \gamma\hat L_{k-1}\Big),\qquad
\Delta_k=\mathrm{clip}\Big(\sqrt{\tau/\hat L_k},\ \Delta_{\min},\ \Delta_{\max}\Big),
\]
with $\tau=0.02$~m, $\Delta_0=0.125$, $\Delta\in[1/16,1]$ and $\gamma=0.5$.
The estimate grows by at most $1/\sqrt\gamma$ per sample and shrinks at once.
The cost is one extra nonlinear rollout per hold, about 1~ms warm for 96 holds.

An earlier variant subtracted twice the observer's worst-case tube radius from
the total innovation. On the noisy campaign that bound explained every
innovation, including 0.27~m, so $\Delta$ rose to its maximum. That variant was
replaced before the final runs.

\section{Diagnoses}
\paragraph{Tire saturation is the abrupt boundary.}
In the noisy 8-m/s turningCrossing, $\Delta$ reached 1 while the model
innovation stayed at 0.1--15~mm. The plan issued at 0.40~s took the full box
and reached 1.10 times the front saturation slip (0.229~rad), while every earlier plan
stayed at or below 0.76. Its own model remainder was then 1.90~m, against 0.10~m one hold
earlier. The estimator shrank $\Delta$ to 0.10 one hold later; the next hold
was infeasible. A reactive estimate learns $L$ only after the jump.

\paragraph{Collision mechanism (noisy 15-m/s brakingLead).}
While passing the braking lead (1.85--2.3~s), the observation innovation was
0.24--0.60~m. From 2.35~s the plans carried positive safety slack (up to 3.7~m);
in the noisy formulation only the observer tightening may use slack, and the
nominal rows are hard. One plan had a 9.9~m model remainder at about
0.3~rad steering. Thereafter every hold restarted from a fresh potential-field
seed with $\Delta$ between 0.07 and 0.15, so each plan stayed close to its
seed. The issued plans steered back toward the lane (lateral position 2.6 to
0.9~m) before the lead was cleared, and the vehicles touched at 3.09~s.

\paragraph{Unrecovered curved test.}
\texttt{clfNominalRecoveryTest} (15~m/s, curvature 0.005, 40~m and 2.5~rad
initial offset) fails: 714 of 715 tests pass. The vehicle comes within
$V\approx0.04$ of cruise at about 7~s and then diverges from about 10~s, well
inside the 200~m reference. The CLF prediction is accurate (predicted 0.30,
nonlinear 0.14 at 10.05~s), and the model innovation is 5--14~mm. The cause is
the anchor. The CLF constrains only the first successor, so each solve moves
only the first input by the full box. The tail stays at the anchor's steering
($-0.046$ to $-0.078$~rad, worsening), and the shifted tail becomes the next
anchor. The required first-step correction outgrows the box, and the CLF slack
grows from 0.01 to 26. In the deleted design, CLF disagreement triggered fresh
seeds that replaced this tail. The test also fails with fixed $\Delta=1$ and
$\Delta=0.125$.

\section{Scope and open items}
No result here is a certificate. The law sets the next step bound only; every
completed solve is issued, and $\tau$ is an empirical target. Open items:
\begin{itemize}
\item an a-priori term for $\hat L$ from the plan's proximity to tire saturation,
  so that $\Delta$ shrinks before a step crosses the knee;
\item tail re-anchoring when the first input is pinned at the box with positive
  CLF slack;
\item the noisy collision: soft observer tightening combined with small-$\Delta$
  fresh seeds;
\item the brakingLead collision/terminal conflict, which existed before.
\end{itemize}
