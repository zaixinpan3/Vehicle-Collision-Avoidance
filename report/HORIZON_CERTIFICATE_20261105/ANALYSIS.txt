Correction to the preceding explanation
---------------------------------------
Shortsighted execution can lead to later infeasibility, but this is different
from saying that the QP at a fixed state becomes feasible if length is increased.
With only prefix constraints, truncating a feasible longer plan gives a shorter
feasible plan. With a terminal inclusion and invariant terminal padding, the
opposite nesting holds for the terminal-reaching feasible domains. The current
runtime enforces only endpoint/prefix constraints, so simply lengthening its
window does not supply the requested guarantee.

Technical result
----------------
CONTROLLER_THEORY.md now states a sufficient direct bound, its induction proof,
and its missing implementation prerequisites. If a common admissible finite-
memory backup keeps whole holds safe on a certified domain and decreases a
nonnegative joint-state rank by a uniform positive amount until an invariant
zero set contained in the terminal set, the ceiling of the worst rank divided
by that decrease is a conservative number of holds. All uncertainty branches
must share the causal policy. Branches that arrive early must remain terminal
under that same continuation. A separate hidden-state controller for each
hypothesis does not establish a common output-feedback bound.

The required rank, decrement, certified domain and compatible backup have NOT
been constructed for the general vehicle/target contract. Constant target A
and beta alone do not imply them, and the optional low-speed terminal component
does not prove finite-time reachability of that component from arbitrary
information sets. No target sign restriction or stopping-distance reserve is
introduced. A resource cap cannot silently truncate a certified required length.

Once a certified feasible terminal-reaching plan exists and conditioning stays
inside its certified successor, its shifted tail provides the next horizon
without a length search. A terminal append can meet a larger minimum horizon.
For the finite NLP, a genuinely feasible nominal point makes the zero SQP step
feasible, provided the lifted state/witness representation and increment bounds
retain it. Accepting a nonzero unverified step can lose the next-frame property.
The nonlinear constraint 1-z^2 <= 0 at z=0 gives the counterexample: feasible
nonlinear points exist at +/-1, but its local linearization is 1 <= 0. A horizon
length formula alone cannot prevent this local infeasibility.
