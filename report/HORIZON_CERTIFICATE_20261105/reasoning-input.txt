Audit a proposed per-frame horizon guarantee for nonlinear vehicle output-feedback MPC. User wants a conservative finite N(I) computed directly, without solving successive horizons, such that lack of prediction length cannot cause infeasibility. Targets have constant tangential acceleration A and sideslip beta, absorbing stop, with zero/positive A allowed. Ego has nonlinear Fiala dynamics and bounded input/slew. Information I is a joint physical-state/controller-memory/previous-input consistency set. Admissible policies are shared u=v+Km, m+=tanh(Am+B phi(Y)+c); coefficients may vary by stage, with actual known root memory. Exact robust problem has whole-hold safety and R_N subset of a certified invariant joint terminal Z_f. Online deployment instead uses finite correlated hypotheses, linearized multiple shooting, one SQP-RTI QP, and currently only endpoint collision/domain constraints (no valid terminal certificate). Do not impose target stopping, a stopping-distance reserve, or a new scene restriction.

Questions: (1) What precisely can and cannot follow about a finite sufficient horizon from the current assumptions? Separate exact terminal-reaching feasibility, avoiding future shortsighted failure, and feasibility of one local QP. Note that without a terminal constraint, shorter exact feasible plans follow by truncation, so longer horizons can reduce feasibility. (2) Audit this sufficient construction: on a certified domain D, an admissible common finite-memory backup policy keeps every complete hold safe; Z_f={V=0} is invariant; every joint successor satisfies V(s+)<=max(V(s)-epsilon,0). Then N(I)=max(1,ceil(sup_I V/epsilon)) suffices, with terminal padding, no online length search. State uniformity, causality, representation and numerical requirements; do not imply V or epsilon is already available. (3) Give the strongest practical path using a stored feasible terminal-reaching tail / offline rank bound, and explain what additional conditions make its warm-start QP feasible. A compact proof and a small counterexample to nonlinear-feasible implies locally-QP-feasible are useful. Avoid claiming a simple TTC/speed formula can solve constrained reachability.
