Latest design and results (October 10, 2026, second report of the day): [Alternative constructions of the terminal set: what enlarges the certified set and what does not](TERMINAL_SET_ALTERNATIVES_20261010.tex) (theory in [../controller/TERMINAL_SAFE_SET.md](../controller/TERMINAL_SAFE_SET.md), Section 2; synthesis in [../controller/NOMINAL_CLF.md](../controller/NOMINAL_CLF.md)).
- Tried on the 8 m/s straight point and then on all four operating points, each as a verified certificate: the ray-averaged (secant) enclosure of the sampled map's Jacobians in place of the pointwise one; a force-level input map (the terminal controller commands the front lateral force and steers through the inverse Fiala curve); a self-consistent growth iteration in place of the fragile fixed-gain final step; Hu-Lin's saturation hull; a poly-quadratic Lyapunov function scheduled on the axle slips; slower decay; one certificate over a speed range; and the polyhedral Lyapunov class against the ellipsoid on one Jacobian vertex set.
- What works: the secant enclosure (the spread of the ray averages is that of the tire curve's secants, two to three times smaller than the tangents'), the force map with it, and the self-consistent growth. What does not: the saturation hull, the poly-quadratic function (its scheduling regression leaves more residual than the principal directions), slower decay, and one quadratic over 8-15 m/s (infeasible). The polytope reaches the box where the ellipsoid gives 0.9 m on the same vertex set, without the residual; making it a certificate needs cone rows and is the most promising next construction.
- Production certificate (after the user's decision to keep the controller's input as steering angle and braking ratio): the linear terminal controller `u = u* + K e` with the secant enclosure and the self-consistent growth (growth 1.3, gain band 0.1, box [2 m, 0.2 rad, 1 m/s, 0.3 m/s, 0.2 rad/s]). Certified sets: 1.49 and 1.59 m of lateral error at 8 m/s (straight, curve) with headings of 0.142 and 0.144 rad, and the 2-m box at 15 m/s with 0.115 rad, against 0.57-0.99 m and 0.040-0.055 rad at `8faf395` and 1.9 m, 0.18 rad for the former unproven linear certificate; hold factors 1.005-1.025. The force-level controller (commit `e2046e0`, superseded) certified 2 m everywhere and was not kept because its steering passes through the tire model's inverse.
- Paired campaigns against `8faf395` under the same load: steering-level design exact 14 of 14 (12), noisy 53 of 84 (39), no collision; CLF-stage conic failures on long plans fall from 18 to 1 encounter, the first-frame horizon from 120-130 to 109 holds. The force-level design measured 13 of 14 and 53 of 84. Full suite 603 of 603.

Previous design and results (October 10, 2026, first report of the day): [Certifying the terminal controller on the nonlinear model](TERMINAL_CONTROLLER_CERTIFICATE_20261010.tex) (theory in [../controller/TERMINAL_SAFE_SET.md](../controller/TERMINAL_SAFE_SET.md), Section 2; synthesis in [../controller/NOMINAL_CLF.md](../controller/NOMINAL_CLF.md)).
- The terminal controller is now the linear feedback `u = u* + K e` whose gain is certified with the CLF on the sampled nonlinear model: a robust LMI over a zonotope enclosure of the one-hold Jacobians along the controller over the set, iterated to self-consistency, with the residual by Petersen's lemma; the former online existence check (hypothesis H3) is replaced by the enclosure hypothesis, checked offline on recorded samples with a margin and re-checked by a unit test on fresh samples. The partial-hold maps give the hold factor by which the tube is inflated between samples.
- The Lipschitz-budget route (`sqrt(rho*) + L <= sqrt(rho)`) fails by two orders of magnitude on this tire model (budget 0.011 against a remainder of about `0.9 sqrt(c)`); a product-box enclosure returns only the trivial set; the enclosure along the controller is what works.
- Certified sets (default box): lateral 0.62/0.57 m, heading 0.053/0.050 rad at 8 m/s (straight/curve) and 0.99/0.69 m, 0.055/0.040 rad at 15 m/s, against 1.9 m and 0.18 rad for the former linear certificate; a slower decay or a wider steering box does not enlarge them (the tire's early saturation sets the size).
- Campaign against commit `deb4dac` under the same load: exact 12 of 14 (14), noisy 39 of 84 (54), no collision. The losses are `clfNoNumericalResult` on longer plans (horizon with a target 81 against 47 holds at the median), the conic solver's known weakness on long plans; the terminal controller itself costs nothing measurable. Full suite 602 of 602.
