RTI constraint correction, November 4, 2026

Request and scope
-----------------
Remove the artificial +/-0.25 increment box on braking feedforward
coefficients while keeping physical input limits. Remove the extra terminal
remaining-braking-travel requirement: this was an implementation assumption,
not a required general terminal condition. No target-motion admission filter
is introduced. Target tangential acceleration and sideslip remain constant,
with the existing absorbing stop at zero speed.

Implementation
--------------
All N policy maps now participate in the sparse QP, including the previously
fixed final braking stage. The appended braking policy is only a numerical
warm-start seed. Braking feedforward coefficients retain their admissible
coefficient bounds but have no additional +/-0.25 increment box. Other
coefficients and separating witnesses retain that numerical step box.
Derived actuator commands retain their magnitude and configured slew rows.
The complete raw block still conditions the joint hypotheses; the committed
memory map is still executed exactly once.

Removed the entire extra stopping-travel terminal proxy, for both stopped /
braking and continuing-target branches, and its witness derivatives. Removed
the unused terminalPreviewSeconds configuration entry. Existing whole-horizon
sampled rectangle constraints include the terminal endpoint, with the same
0.1 m clearance plus 0.2 m engineering intersample reserve. Physical-domain
rows also include the endpoint. There is no replacement invariant terminal
certificate in this deployment. The conditional exact theorem and optional
low-speed certificate remain documented; the offline lowSpeedReserve helper
and comparator algorithms are unchanged. Endpoint constraints alone do not
supply terminal invariance or recursive feasibility.

Verification
------------
233 tests passed across dynamicOutputFeedbackTest (30 results), configuration,
source-budget, Fiala tire, road-load, RTI model consistency, terminal safe-set,
and controller input/geometry tests. No failed or incomplete tests. The full
repository suite was not run. tests.csv records the individual results.
Initial pre-regression dynamic-output-feedback run: 27 passed, recorded in
initial-tests.csv. Final focused rerun: 30 passed after strengthening the
actuator-limit infeasibility assertions to require solver exit -2, recorded
in final-dynamic-tests.csv.

New regressions force a -0.35 root brake coefficient increment from -0.6:
its linear problem is feasible. A -0.5 increment (command -1.1) is infeasible;
with braking slew limited to 1/s, the -0.35 increment is also infeasible.
Another regression checks that the final optimized policy is not fixed to
-0.6. A 25 m/s, 15 m stationary-lead fixture now admits a QP with terminal
speed above 20 m/s, without imposing stopping travel on the endpoint.
Existing overlap rejection, sensor causality, target persistence, and nonlinear
certificate disclaimers continue to pass.

Closed-loop experiments
-----------------------
Default four-stage horizon (0.2 s), one memory coordinate, two prediction
substeps per hold, zero future-noise direction, three initial correlated
hypotheses, seed 20261104. Actual hold 0.05 s, raw sensors every 0.0125 s.
The independent ode45 plant uses relative tolerance 1e-10 and absolute tolerance
1e-12, with 41 clearance checks per hold. Startup virtual warmup is enabled;
its commands are not applied to the actual plant. Each case requests 60 holds.
See table.csv and the per-case JSON files for all measurements and failures.

8 m/s headOn, acceleratingHeadOn, curvedHeadOn: 23 holds completed, then QP
failure at t=1.15 s, versus 13, 12, 13 holds in the prior version. The prior
failure time is passed in each case; this does not establish recursive
feasibility. IntersectionCrossing completes 32 holds then fails at t=1.60 s.
StoppedLead and hardBrakingLead at 8 m/s complete all 60 requested holds.

25 m/s stoppedLead and hardBrakingLead: initialization now succeeds and 20
holds complete before failure at t=1.00 s; both previously failed initialization.
No executed hold in these runs has a negative measured clearance. The run
ends at QP failure; no claim is made about plant safety after that point or
about longer encounters in cases that complete the requested 60 holds.

There are 261 successful controller calls, of which 253 are continuation calls.
All successful continuation calls are below 50 ms (maximum 43.051 ms).
Two of eight recorded initialization calls exceed 50 ms (maximum 66.704 ms).
One of six failing calls exceeds 50 ms (53.621 ms). These measurements exclude
startup virtual warmup and do not establish a worst-case realtime guarantee.

Remaining-failure audit
-----------------------
The new audit replays all six failing histories with exactly matching commands
(maximum discrepancy zero), then exports the current QP. Removing only the
collision inequalities makes all six linear programs feasible. Removing the
remaining numerical step boxes does not make any feasible (all six exit -2).
An elastic LP that changes only collision rows has a positive optimal common
slack in all six cases: 0.446725 m headOn, 0.208863 m acceleratingHeadOn,
0.366425 m curvedHeadOn, 0.0255584 m intersectionCrossing, 0.543662 m
25 m/s stoppedLead, and 0.116798 m 25 m/s hardBrakingLead. Primal residuals
are below 7e-10. The ordinary feasibility LP returns -9 for two cases;
those numerical statuses are inconclusive on their own. The successful
positive-slack optimizations provide separate evidence of local conflict.
These diagnostics never execute relaxed commands. Collision constraints
remain hard in the runtime. Linearized separating-plane slack is not an exact
physical collision-distance deficit or proof of unavoidable collision.

With only 0.2 s of prediction and endpoint constraints, these tests expose
myopic behavior: the 25 m/s vehicles remain near 24.90 m/s when the QP first
fails. A terminal continuation design or anticipatory planning change would
be a further algorithmic change; the deleted stopping-distance proxy is not
silently reintroduced. No global nonlinear maneuver search was performed.
