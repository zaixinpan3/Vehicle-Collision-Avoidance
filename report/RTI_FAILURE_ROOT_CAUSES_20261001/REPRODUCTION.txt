Root causes of the RTI controller's scenario failures -- October 1, 2026

Controller under study: commit 5f7497718d4774a2515330650ee8815ccfa67b20
(real-time iteration with flow reinitialization). The analysis ran on a frozen
copy at /home/zai/.cache/collisionAvoidance/rti-failure-analysis-20261001/source
whose controller, configuration, driver, scenario and native kernel files are
byte-identical to that commit (source.sha256 and manifest.json). The copy was
taken from the working tree before the commit; the hashes were rechecked
against the commit afterwards. MATLAB R2026a, -singleCompThread, Optimization
and Control System Toolboxes, Coder kernels in solver/controller.

1. Campaign (methods/campaign.m), four partitions run concurrently:
     matlab -singleCompThread -batch "part=P;parts=4;run('<raw>/campaign.m')"
   with P = 0..3. Each case calls
     runNonlinearPredictiveSafetyValidation(Scenarios=NAME,Frames=1600,
       ControllerConfiguration=struct('referenceSpeed',S,'controller',struct('horizonSteps',N)),
       RequireCollisionThreat=true,RecoveryDwellSeconds=5,OutputFile=..,ContinuationFile=..)
   for S/N = 8/8 and 15/16 and the seven collisionThreatScenario fixtures.
   Exact state, known target motion, no road constraints, no noise or random
   draws. campaign-outcomes.csv is produced by methods/summarize.py.
   The issued inputs were compared frame by frame with the independent campaign
   of report/RTI_FLOW_REINITIALIZATION_20261001 (same source): all 14 cases
   agree bitwise over their common frames, with identical minimum clearances.

2. Instrumented replays (methods/replayCase.m via runReplay.m/runReplays.m)
   rerun a case with the unchanged controller and the driver's own fixture,
   target conversion and ODE45 step, and store each frame's issued input,
   returned affine plan, linearization anchor, stage exits and CLF values.
   Replayed inputs reproduce the campaign. Full 80-s replays exist for the
   eleven non-recovered cases and the three recovered ones.
   trust-box-statistics.csv: methods/exportTables.m (frames at t >= 10 s; a
   box edge means |u - anchor| > 0.0749 rad steering or > 0.1249 braking).

3. Single-call ablations (methods/ablateFrame.m via runAblation.m) rebuild a
   captured call with methods/rtiInternals.m, generated by
     python3 methods/makeInternals.py controller/solvePredictiveControl.m rtiInternals.m
   which copies every local function unchanged and only adds constraint-row
   labels to localFormulate. Each rebuilt call must reproduce the issued input
   exactly (column exactReproduction). For the three restart calls the issued
   input comes from the flow restart; there the rebuilt warm attempt instead
   reproduces the controller's warm PCBF exit -2 (variant "original"). Variants
   change bounds, rows or cones of that one problem and repeat the two stages
   with the controller's options. single-call-ablations.csv.

4. Collision holds (methods/collisionTable.m, collisionDetail.m,
   collisionHold.m): 5,001-point ODE45 solutions of the issued hold,
   separating-axis penetration depth, and the existing offline
   predictiveSafetyGeometry.intervalClearance on the node-to-node interpolant.
   collision-holds.csv.

5. Counterfactual controllers (variants/*.patch) are copies of
   controller/solvePredictiveControl.m placed first on the MATLAB path by
   methods/replayCaseCF.m / replayCaseCF2.m. They are diagnostics, not
   proposed algorithms. Drivers: runCounterfactual.m, runPostCounterfactual.m,
   runCollisionCounterfactual.m (margin variants change only
   collision.safetyMarginMeters). counterfactuals.csv
   (methods/exportCounterfactuals.m); recovery uses the driver's tolerances,
   minimum time and dwell, computed from the replayed successor errors.
   The first free-steering attempt (replayCaseCF) aborted in the plant
   integrator without a record; it was rerun with replayCaseCF2, which
   records plant and controller failures.

6. Restart calls (methods/exportRestart.m, restartDetail.m): geometry of the
   shifted-input rollout at the three warm PCBF failures.
   restart-geometry.csv.

Concurrency and timing: campaign partitions, replays and counterfactuals ran
concurrently with each other and with unrelated jobs on a 16-core machine.
No reported campaign call reached the 5-s budget (maximum 1.31 s), so the
campaign outcome is load independent. The third-stage counterfactual reached
the budget in 18-25 calls per 80-s run and used those finite points, so its
details can depend on load. Wall times here are not benchmarks.

Files not committed: campaign JSON/MAT, replay MATs, captured calls,
counterfactual records and the frozen source stay under the raw directory;
