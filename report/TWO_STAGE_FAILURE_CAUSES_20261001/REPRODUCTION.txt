runProbe uses MaxIterations=400, ConstraintTolerance=1e-8,
OptimalityTolerance=1e-7 and MaxTime=5 s per diagnostic solve; original campaign
has the shared five-second soft controller budget. All original failure flags
were reproduced. Trials are diagnostic modifications, not controller changes.

The two-input CLF relaxation drops all future constraints, retains first-step
linear dynamics and input boxes, and minimizes the resulting convex quadratic
on that box. It evaluates the stationary point and edge optima. Its minimum is
a lower bound on the complete CLF problem. Returned candidates are checked
against every original cone, equality, inequality and bound.

Farkas witnesses satisfy A' mu + E' nu approximately zero, mu nonnegative,
sum(mu)=1 and b' mu + rhs' nu negative. The saved JSON lists positive supports;
full matrices and multipliers are in cache linear-certificates.mat.
